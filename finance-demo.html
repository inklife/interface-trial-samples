About this study

A calmer close.

Common Ledger is an original, self-contained interface concept for finance operations. Its layout separates settled movement, pending transfers and exceptions so a reader can understand what each number means.

All names, dates, references and amounts are fictional. Filtering, chart views, transfer details and CSV export work locally with a preset dataset. The page does not connect to a bank, wallet, payment provider or analytics service. It stores no personal information and makes no network requests.
